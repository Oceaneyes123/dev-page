## 3. App-Specific Privacy Practices

### TrialShield — Trial & Subscription Tracker

**Package Name:** `com.jezreldave.trialshield`

TrialShield helps users manage free trials, track subscriptions, schedule renewal reminders, and access subscription cancellation information.

#### 3.1 Information Stored on Your Device

TrialShield may store the following information when you create or manage subscriptions:

- Subscription or service names.
- Free trial expiration and renewal dates.
- Subscription prices, currencies, and billing cycles.
- Billing method categories.
- Reminder preferences and notification settings.
- Cancellation links, statuses, notes, and confirmation references.
- Related application identifiers and locally maintained reminder records.

This information is stored locally on your Android device.

TrialShield does not require an online account and does not upload subscription records to JD Software Lab servers.

#### 3.2 Optional Notification Access

TrialShield provides an optional feature that helps identify notifications related to free trials and subscriptions.

If you explicitly enable Android Notification Access:

- Android may provide TrialShield access to notifications from other applications, including notification text and the originating application.
- TrialShield examines notification information locally to identify possible trial or subscription activity.
- Notifications categorized as messages, calls, alarms, and certain other unrelated categories are excluded from further processing.
- Raw notification text is processed temporarily in device memory and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to our servers.
- Subscription information identified from notifications is saved only when you choose to track it.

Notification Access is optional. You can disable it at any time through your Android device settings.

#### 3.3 Shared Text and Manual Entry

You may manually enter subscription information or share text from another application with TrialShield.

Shared text is processed locally to identify relevant subscription details. Information is saved to TrialShield's local database only when you choose to save it.

#### 3.4 Reminders and Device Permissions

TrialShield may use the following Android capabilities:

- **Notification permission:** To display subscription and renewal reminders.
- **Exact alarm access:** To schedule time-sensitive reminders.
- **Device restart and time-change events:** To help maintain reminder schedules.
- **Notification listener access:** To identify potential subscription-related notifications when explicitly enabled.

These features operate locally on your device.

You can manage supported permissions in Android settings.

#### 3.5 Data Collection, Sharing, and Advertising

The current version of TrialShield:

- Does not require account registration.
- Does not transmit your subscription records to our servers.
- Does not use third-party advertising SDKs.
- Does not use third-party analytics SDKs.
- Does not sell or share your locally stored subscription records with advertisers.
- Does not require the Android Internet permission.

If these practices change in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures before introducing the changes.

#### 3.6 Backups and External Services

TrialShield supports manual import and export of subscription records using JSON files.

When you export data, you choose where the backup file is saved using Android's file picker. Backup files may contain your subscription details in readable form.

If you choose a cloud storage provider or another external destination, that provider's privacy practices apply to information stored there.

TrialShield may also open external websites when you follow subscription cancellation links. Those websites operate independently and are governed by their respective privacy policies.

#### 3.7 Data Retention and Deletion

TrialShield retains saved subscription records and preferences on your device until you delete them.

You can remove individual subscription entries or use the application's **Delete All Local Data** function to erase saved subscriptions, reminder history, and application preferences.

Uninstalling TrialShield also removes its application-managed local data under normal Android behavior.

Backup files that you manually exported are not automatically deleted. You are responsible for managing copies saved outside the application.

Because TrialShield does not use online accounts or store subscription records on JD Software Lab servers, there is no separate cloud account or server-side subscription database to delete.
